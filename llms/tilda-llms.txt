# Tilda Research

> Tilda Research (tilda.bio) deploys AI Teammates that perform the operational work of clinical trials for sponsors, CROs, and research sites — automating study workflows, data capture, trial monitoring, and regulatory compliance. Founded by Ram Yalamanchili and backed by 8VC. Its product, Tilda Sense, is a clinical-research platform (sense.tilda.bio); the company publishes no public developer program or API documentation.
